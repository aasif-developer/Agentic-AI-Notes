========================================
ABOUT C3
========================================

C3 stands for Campus to Corporate Club.

C3 is a student-focused initiative that helps students develop
technical skills, soft skills, practical knowledge, teamwork,
communication skills and career readiness.

The main goal of C3 is to help students bridge the gap between
college learning and the requirements of the corporate world.


========================================
PURPOSE OF C3
========================================

C3 focuses on:

- Technical Skill Development
- Soft Skill Development
- Practical Learning
- Teamwork
- Communication
- Problem Solving
- Career Readiness
- Industry Preparation


========================================
DOMAINS
========================================

C3 provides different domains that allow students to explore
and develop technical and professional skills.

Students can choose domains based on their interests and
career goals.


========================================
AIML DOMAIN
========================================

AIML stands for Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

The AIML domain helps students learn about:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Python
- Data Handling
- Deep Learning
- Generative AI
- Practical AI Applications

The AIML domain focuses on helping students understand AI/ML
concepts and develop practical skills through learning and
projects.
