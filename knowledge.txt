STUDENT GUIDANCE CELL (SGC)
========================================

ABOUT SGC
---------
Student Guidance Cell (SGC) is a student-led organization at
C. Abdul Hakeem College of Engineering and Technology (CAHCET),
Melvisharam, Tamil Nadu.

Established: 2010

Mission:
Empower students through technical workshops, career guidance,
leadership, innovation, and professional development.

Motto:
"For the Students, Of the Students, By the Students."

========================================

WHAT SGC DOES
-------------

SGC organizes:

- Technical Workshops
- AI Workshops

- Career Guidance Sessions
- Placement Preparation
